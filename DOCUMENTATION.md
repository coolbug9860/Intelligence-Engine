# KAISO Intelligence: Strategic Research OS
## Technical Manual & Analytical Framework v5.0

---

## 1. Executive Summary
KAISO is a high-fidelity **Strategic Intelligence Operating System** designed for B2B research firms and institutional analysts. Unlike traditional news aggregators, KAISO utilizes an **Asymmetric Reasoning Engine** to synthesize raw signals into deterministic, investment-grade research opportunities. 

The system moves beyond "prediction" into **causal estimation**, providing clear provenance and stress-tested resilience for every suggested market movement.

---

## 2. Core Architectural Pillars

### I. Deterministic Orchestration
The system follows a strict logical flow: **Signal Extraction** -> **Semantic Clustering** -> **Causal Tracing** -> **Resilience Testing**. No intelligence is generated without a primary evidence anchor.

### II. The Asymmetric Reasoning Engine (ARE)
ARE identifies correlations that are non-obvious to standard LLMs. It looks for "Nexus Connections"—where a regulatory shift in one sector (e.g., Singaporean shipping) creates a value-capture opportunity in a distant sector (e.g., European Green Hydrogen).

### III. Veracity Defense
Every signal is weighted by its **Source Authority**. Reuters or WSJ reports carry higher "Inertia" than generic blogs, impacting the final Intelligence Profile.

## 3. The Analytical Interface: Component Guide

### A. Strategic Telemetry Feed (Left Column)
This is your **Raw Signal Ingress**. It monitors high-authority RSS feeds in real-time.
- **Signal Identification**: Green indicators represent "Fresh" signals ingested within the current session.
- **Source Integrity**: Hover over any signal to see the raw provenance and original publication metadata.

### B. The Intelligence Matrix (Central Grid)
The matrix displays **AI-Derived Research Opportunities**. These are NOT just news summaries; they are synthesized business cases.
- **Score (1.0 - 10.0)**: A composite measure of commercial viability.
- **Nexus Indicator**: Appears when a theme is corroborated by multiple independent high-authority signals.
- **Compact Intelligence Profile**: The sparklines on each card represent the three-part confidence model (Evidence, Resilience, Integrity).

### C. The Deep-Dive Dossier (Expanded View)
When an opportunity is selected, the system generates a high-fidelity brief containing:

#### 1. The Intelligence Profile
Replaces binary "confidence" with:
- **Evidence Weight**: How much raw grounding data exists?
- **Systemic Resilience**: Will this trend survive if a major driver (e.g., a subsidy) is removed?
- **Calibration Integrity**: How direct is the logical link from event to profit?

#### 2. Causal Intelligence Trace
A visualized 4-node logic chain showing the "Domino Effect":
- **Trigger** (The Event) → **1st Order** (Direct Impact) → **2nd Order** (Market Ripple) → **Value Capture** (Monetization).

#### 3. Structural Stress Simulation
Real-time "What If" scenarios testing the opportunity against **Regulatory Pivots**, **Supply Chain Volatility**, and **Economic Contraction**.

#### 4. Intelligence Evolution (30D)
A trajectory map showing the **Consensus Velocity**. Surging sparklines indicate "Rising Stars," while flat lines indicate "Established Norms."

## 4. Operational Workflow for Analysts

1. **Signal Surveillance**: Review the Telemetry Feed for emerging patterns.
2. **Opportunity Selection**: Target cards with **Nexus** badges and a Score of **8.5+**.
3. **Resilience Verification**: Open the detail card and inspect the **Market Stress-Test**. If "Regulatory Resilience" is below 40%, the opportunity is high-risk.
4. **Causal Validation**: Walk through the **Causal Trace** to ensure the "Value Capture" logic aligns with your firm's commercial capabilities.
5. **Brief Generation**: Use the "Generate Full Brief" tool to synthesize an AI-powered whitepaper draft derived from the current signal batch.

## 6. Technical Integrity Notice
KAISO runs a two-model pipeline on the `@google/genai` SDK:
- **Signal analysis** uses **Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite** — reliable free-tier capacity that returns the full structured portfolio every run.
- **Brief generation** uses **Gemini 3.5 Flash** at the **HIGH** thinking level — near-Pro reasoning at Flash-tier cost and free-tier quota, used for the one-off report commission document.

The AI synthesis stage is generative and therefore not bit-for-bit reproducible between runs. Everything *after* synthesis — scoring, resilience, freshness, deduplication, diversity, forecasting, and trajectory math — is deterministic for a given signal batch.
